System rules used for the available vehicle list
  1. Only vehicles with no overlapping booking, maintenance or hold in the Fleet Planner are offered.
  2. Vehicles based at the pickup location are listed first (then other branches — transfer required).
  3. Same category → allocate directly. Different category or dates → sent to the customer as an offer to accept.
  4. Pickup and dropoff times must be between 08:00 and 17:15 in 15-minute intervals.
