Help

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Our team is one click away.

What do I need to pick up a vehicle?

Bring your valid driver's licence and the card used for the booking. Your booking reference is in your confirmation and in My Bookings.

Can I change my pick-up time?

Yes. Sign in to My Bookings and choose "Request Change". Our team reviews the request and you'll see the update in the portal as soon as it's approved.

What if the vehicle I want isn't available?

Your request goes straight to our operations team, who will offer you the closest alternative vehicle or time. You can accept the offer from My Bookings.

Can I return the vehicle to a different location?

Use the ⇄ button next to "Pick up" in the search form to choose a different drop-off branch.

How do I talk to someone about my booking?

Use the chat button at the bottom of the page. If you're signed in, you can pick the booking you want to talk about so the agent has all the details.
