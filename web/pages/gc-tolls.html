TOLLS — DAILY REPORT, CHARGED OVERNIGHT
How it works Every day we upload the toll report here. Each toll is matched to the booking by rego and time, and charged automatically overnight to the card on the booking. We only step in when a toll has no booking (Assign) or a card declines. Toll policy Tolls are not included in the rental. Each toll is charged at cost plus a $3.95 administration fee. Tolls can take up to 7 days to reach GC Tech. If the card declines, a $6.50 reprocessing fee applies per unpaid toll instead of the $3.95. In debt collection, a $15.00 fee is added per outstanding toll. Customers can use their own toll tag or a temporary Linkt pass.

Demo data: tolls are generated from the demo hires and the uploaded file is not read. The new system does the same as today: upload, match by rego and time, charge overnight.
